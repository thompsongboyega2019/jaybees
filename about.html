About Us

Crafting Sweet Memories,
One Layer at a Time

Jaybees Confectionery was founded with a singular, joyful mission: to transform life's most meaningful moments into exquisite, edible art.

What started as an artisan home kitchen fueled by curiosity and flour dust has blossomed into one of the most beloved boutique cake studios. Over the years, we have had the honor of being the centerpiece at hundreds of weddings, milestone birthdays, baby showers, and grand corporate celebrations.

We firmly believe that a cake should never be an afterthought. It is the focal point of your photographs, the sweet finale of your celebration, and the sensory memory that your guests take home with them.

The Pillars of Jaybees Excellence

Every layer, filling, and decorative swirl adheres to our unyielding standards of quality and artistic innovation.

Pure Natural Ingredients

We strictly use pure butter, farm-fresh organic eggs, Belgian couverture chocolate, and whole Madagascar vanilla beans.

Artistic Innovation

From hyper-realistic sugar flowers to gravity-defying architectural tiers, we merge traditional craftsmanship with trendy couture design.

Immaculate Hygiene

Our temperature-controlled kitchen adheres to the strictest food sanitation and allergen cross-contact prevention protocols.

Personalized Service

We work one-on-one with you to understand your color palette, moodboard, flavor preferences, and event timeline.
