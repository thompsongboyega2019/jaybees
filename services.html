Signature Sponges & Gourmet Fillings

Every layer is made with scratch-baked moisture, premium Belgian chocolates, and house-made curds.

Classic Favorites

  • Madagascar Vanilla Bean: Infused with whole vanilla pods and white chocolate cream.
  • Belgian Dark Chocolate Fudge: Deep 70% cocoa sponge layered with silky ganache.
  • Red Velvet Supreme: Traditional tender crumb paired with creamy Madagascar vanilla frosting.

Fruity & Zesty

  • Lemon Raspberry Symphony: Tart Meyer lemon sponge with house-cooked raspberry compote.
  • Passionfruit Mango Swirl: Tropical curd nestled between airy sponge layers.
  • Strawberry Champagne Luxe: Real strawberry reductions with sparkling champagne cream.

Specialty Gourmet

  • Salted Caramel Biscoff: Brown butter sponge layered with speculoos crunch.
  • Pistachio Rose Cardamom: Roasted Persian pistachio sponge with delicate rose water.
  • Espresso Hazelnut Mocha: Rich espresso soak with crunchy hazelnut praline paste.
